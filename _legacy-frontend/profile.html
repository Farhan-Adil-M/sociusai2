Where this information lives

Your answers — spoken during the intro or typed here — are stored in this app's database and used to personalise your learning: how the tutor explains things, which examples it picks and how hard your first quizzes start.

They are never shared. There is no sign-in and no separate accounts: this installation has one shared profile, like all your study data here. If the server is reset (for example a fresh deployment), the profile disappears with it — you can always rebuild it on this page, and you can delete it yourself at any time.

Danger zone

Restart the intro

Removes the saved profile and starts the tutor's questions from the beginning. Quizzes and materials are untouched.

Delete saved profile

Only your answers and the tutor's guesses are removed. Quizzes, flashcard decks and uploaded material are NOT deleted.
